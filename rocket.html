FrostSMP Rocket

How it works

Choose a wager and launch the rocket. The multiplier continuously increases while the rocket climbs. Cash out before the predetermined crash point to receive your wager multiplied by the cashout multiplier.

Fairness

  • Every round generates one cryptographically secure random value.
  • The crash point is generated before the animation starts.
  • Bet size does not affect the result.
  • Balance does not affect the result.
  • Previous rounds do not affect the result.
  • Cashout history does not affect the result.
  • Every round is independent.
crash = (1 − 0.03) / u

where u is a secure random value between 0 and 1. The target mathematical house edge is approximately 3%.

Payout

Payout = Bet × Cashout Multiplier

A crash before cashout loses the wager. Cashout is accepted only while the round is in the flying state.

Limits

Minimum wager: 1,000
