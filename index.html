Rocket rules

The rocket starts at 1.00x and rises continuously. Each round's crash point is fixed before launch.

  • Minimum wager $1,000 · maximum $1,000,000.
  • Higher multipliers are progressively less likely to be reached.
  • Cash out before the crash to receive wager × multiplier.
  • If the rocket crashes first, the wager is lost.
  • Previous rounds never affect future rounds.
